Resolved safety findings

  • Profitability now compares gross proceeds against all costs plus the required margin; a loss cannot pass a zero-profit threshold.
  • Fees and seller payout are derived from the exact Basic, regular, or Advanced Seaport fulfillment generated by OpenSea.
  • Legacy, manual, and EIP-1559 fee settings are honored for approval and sale transactions.
  • Approval gas is estimated before approval and its confirmed gas cost is used in the final decision.
  • All payment, offer, and native-gas currencies are repriced immediately before broadcast.
  • USDG and non-ETH native currencies require explicit prices; no stablecoin peg is assumed silently.
  • Approval and sale receipts have a timeout, Ctrl+C handling, confirmation depth, and canonical receipt revalidation.
  • Simulation and --dry-run can validate an owned existing token through offer lookup, fulfillment, fees, approvals, gas, and profitability without broadcasting.
